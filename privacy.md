# Privacy Policy — The Botanical Eye

**Last updated:** 10 October 2026

This policy explains what The Botanical Eye ("the app") does with personal data. The app
identifies plants from a photo, checks their health, keeps a garden of your plants on your
phone and reminds you to care for them.

We comply with the EU General Data Protection Regulation (**GDPR**) and Spanish data
protection law.

---

## 1. Who is responsible for your data

- **Controller:** Aissa Bamogo Redondo (sole trader, Spain)
- **Contact:** aissa.b.code@gmail.com

---

## 2. You do not create an account

The app has no sign-up, no login and no password. On first launch it creates a **random
identifier** for your installation (a UUID). Its first eight characters are shown in the app
under **Profile › Your ID**, for example `3FA8-5F64`.

We never ask for, and never receive, your email address or phone number.

---

## 3. What stays on your phone

The following is stored only on your device and never uploaded to our servers:

- **Your garden:** the plant photos you save, plant names, diagnoses, care tips and progress
  history.
- **Your reminders:** they are scheduled as local notifications by your phone.
- The name you type in your profile, your language, and your weekly free-scan counter.

Everything in this section is removed when you uninstall the app or clear its storage.

---

## 4. What we store on our servers

Our backend runs on Supabase. Attached to your installation's random identifier we store:

- **Installation record:** the identifier, the platform (iOS or Android), and when the app
  was first and last opened.
- **Usage counters:** how many plant analyses the identifier made each day, and a daily count
  keyed by a **one-way hash of your IP address** (we never store the address itself). These
  limit abuse and are deleted after about a week.
- **Free access from us:** if we give you free Premium, the name we noted for you and the
  start and end dates.
- **Partner codes:** if you redeem a partner's code — for example an influencer's
  "1 month free" code — we record which partner it was and the amounts of the payments that
  follow, so that we can pay them a commission on your subscription for up to 12 months. The
  partner only ever sees totals: how many people used their code and what they have earned.
  Never who you are.

We do **not** store the plant photos you scan.

---

## 5. Artificial intelligence

The app uses artificial intelligence — **Google's Gemini models** — to identify plants,
assess their health and write care advice.

- **Every identification, diagnosis and care tip is generated by AI**, not by a person. No
  human reviews them before you see them.
- **AI can make mistakes.** Never rely on the app to decide whether a plant is safe to eat,
  touch, or keep near children or pets. Some plants are toxic. Ask a qualified professional
  before acting on anything that matters.
- **No automated decisions about you.** The app makes no decisions with legal or similarly
  significant effects (GDPR Art. 22).
- We do **not** use your photos or results to train AI models.

---

## 6. Plant photos and AI analysis

When you scan a plant, the photo is sent from the app to our server, which forwards it to
**Google's Gemini API** together with your language and, when you track a saved plant's
progress, the earlier diagnoses of that plant. The result comes back to your phone. The photo
is held only for the duration of that request and is never written to storage by us.

Before the first photo leaves your phone, the app tells you this, names Google as the
recipient and asks for your permission. If you choose "Not now", nothing is sent.

Google processes the request under its own terms: <https://ai.google.dev/gemini-api/terms>
and <https://policies.google.com/privacy>. We use Gemini's paid service, under which Google
does not use the content to improve its products.

> Photograph only the plant. Anything else in the picture — a face, a document, the inside of
> your home — is sent along with it.

| Purpose | Legal basis (GDPR) |
| --- | --- |
| Analysing the photos you submit | Performance of a contract, Art. 6(1)(b) |
| Usage limits and abuse prevention | Legitimate interest in keeping the service available, Art. 6(1)(f) |
| Giving free Premium to people we choose | Legitimate interest, Art. 6(1)(f) |
| Paying commission to the partner whose code you redeemed | Legitimate interest in running the referral programme, Art. 6(1)(f) |
| Tax and accounting records | Legal obligation, Art. 6(1)(c) |

We do **not** use your data for advertising or profiling, and we do **not** sell it.

- **Apple** and **Google Play** — distribute the app and take payment. We never see your
  payment details. <https://www.apple.com/legal/privacy/> ·
  <https://policies.google.com/privacy>

There are no advertising SDKs and no third-party analytics in the app.

## 10. How long we keep it

- **Plant photos:** not kept by us at all — only for the seconds the analysis takes.
- **Usage counters and IP hashes:** about one week.
- **Installation record and free-access grants:** until you ask us to delete them.
- **Partner-code records:** for as long as tax and accounting law requires.
- **Backups:** deleted data disappears from rolling backups within 30 days.
- **Data on your phone:** until you clear the app's storage or uninstall it.

---

## 11. Your rights

Under GDPR you can ask us for a copy of your data, to correct or delete it, to restrict or
object to how we use it, or to receive it in a portable format.

Email **aissa.b.code@gmail.com** and include **your ID** from Profile › Your ID, so we can
find your records. We answer within 30 days.

You may also complain to the Spanish data protection authority, the **AEPD**
(<https://www.aepd.es>), or to your local supervisory authority.

---

## 12. Children

The app is for a general audience and is not directed at children. We do not knowingly
collect personal data from children under 14. If you believe we hold such data, email
**aissa.b.code@gmail.com** and we will delete it.

---

## 13. Security

- TLS encryption for everything in transit.
- Database tables locked with Row-Level Security; the app can reach only the functions it needs.
- All provider keys held server-side; none are readable from the app.

No system is perfectly secure. If a breach affects your data we will notify you and the
supervisory authority as GDPR Art. 33–34 require.
